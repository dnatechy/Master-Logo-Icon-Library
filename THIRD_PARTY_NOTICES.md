## Presentation variants

Files in folders such as `Transparent_Black`, `Transparent_White`, `Circle_Blue`, `Circle_Dark`, and rounded-square variants are convenience transformations generated from source geometry. A transformed logo is **not automatically an approved brand treatment**. When a brand's rules prohibit recolouring, framing or alteration, use its raw/original asset instead.
